# Practice attempts are append-only learning evidence

Submitted Practice Attempts will be immutable and retained as append-only learning evidence; another answer creates another attempt, while regrading or correction creates a linked Attempt Evaluation instead of overwriting the original record. Mastery and review policy will derive from this evidence rather than accepting direct model-written mastery state, trading additional records for explainability and future policy recalculation without making the rest of the system event-sourced.
